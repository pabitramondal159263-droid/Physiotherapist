❓ Frequently Asked Questions

In most cases, you do not need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist. However, if you are using health insurance to cover the cost, some insurance providers may require a referral. We recommend checking with your specific provider before your first visit.

Please wear loose, comfortable clothing that allows the physiotherapist to easily access the area being treated. For example, if you are coming in for knee pain, please bring or wear shorts. For shoulder or back issues, a tank top or loose t-shirt is ideal.

Your initial assessment will typically take about 60 minutes to allow for a comprehensive evaluation. Follow-up treatment sessions usually last between 30 to 45 minutes, depending on your personalized treatment plan.
